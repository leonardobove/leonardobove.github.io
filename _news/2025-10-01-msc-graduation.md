Graduated from the University of Pisa with a M.Sc. in Electronic Engineering (Embedded Systems & Mechatronics), scoring **110/110 summa cum laude**. My thesis, *"Superconducting qubit readout and control system based on FPGA and development of a pulse sequencer,"* was advised by Professor Massimo Macucci, Professor Stefano Di Pascoli, and Dr. David Van Zanten, and grew out of my master's thesis project at SQMS, Fermilab.
