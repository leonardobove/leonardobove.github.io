QPCB (Quantum Pulse Conversion Board) is a custom pulse frequency conversion board I designed and analyzed using microwave EM simulations (ADS Keysight) as part of my master's thesis project at SQMS, Fermilab, in support of the QICK-based pulse sequencer (Qubase) used to characterize 2D and 3D superconducting qubits.
